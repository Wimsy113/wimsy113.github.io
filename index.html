About

Ten-plus years of
writing code and
breaking grids.

I've spent my career on both sides of the screen — as a software developer and as a UX designer — which mostly means I've never met a hand-off I couldn't argue with. I've designed and built for humanitarian water-tracking in the field, disaster-prep tools people reach for on their worst day, fintech built to protect aging and disabled adults, and programs helping veterans land in tech.

The through-line: high-stakes moments, real people, and interfaces that have to earn their keep. Outside of that, I'm drawing fanged little monsters, arguing about design systems, and generally refusing to make anything boring.
