01 — Overview

Project overview

ResiliencePoint is a platform for emergency managers (EMs) to gather insights about their communities, hazards, and outreach effectiveness. It was developed as a successor to EM-Dash to provide a more actionable, clear, and human-centered interface for interpreting data and making decisions. The platform needed to balance rich datasets with simplicity, enabling EMs to quickly understand local conditions and adapt their outreach strategies.

02 — Design Process & Development

Understanding EM workflows

Our process began by analyzing EM workflows and needs.

User Research

Interviews and observations revealed that EMs needed rapid access to key metrics: population counts, hazard activity, and prep check engagement in their jurisdiction. They required clear ways to search, filter, and visualize data for actionable insights.

Wireframing & Prototyping

Pages were designed with multiple graphs and tables, navigated via a top menu bar. The goal was to give EMs access to core metrics while keeping all visualizations immediately accessible.

Feature Planning

Based on the PRD, features included:

  • Prep check engagement summaries
  • Hazard activity heatmaps
  • Recent incident or insight feeds
  • Search tied to individual graphs/tables
Wireframe showing top menu and multiple component layouts

Testing & Observations

  • The top navigation created cognitive overload; users had difficulty understanding hierarchy.
  • Component-level search required repeated queries for each chart or table, slowing workflow.
  • Critical insights were often buried and not visible at a glance.
  • Accessibility issues appeared: some text contrast, alignment, and hierarchy made rapid comprehension challenging.
03 — The Pivot

Discovering usability issues

Through usability and accessibility testing, it became clear the design needed a rethink:

  • Navigation via a top menu was overwhelming and visually cluttered.
  • Component-specific search caused inefficiencies and inconsistent workflows.
  • Key insights were difficult to locate quickly.

This led to a redesign that emphasized clarity, hierarchy, and workflow efficiency, while addressing accessibility concerns.

04 — Redesign & Enhancements

What changed, and why

1. Side Menu Navigation

  • Replaced top menu with a vertical side menu to reduce clutter and establish clear hierarchy.
  • Allowed more space for dashboards and summary insights.
Redesigned side menu

2. Overview Section

  • Added a concise overview highlighting the most important metrics: total population, hazard activity, and prep check engagement.
  • Brought key insights to the forefront for EMs to consume at a glance.
Overview section screenshot

3. Page-Wide Search Integration

  • Moved search from individual components to a platform-wide search on the main page.
  • Improved workflow efficiency and clarity.
Integrated search example

4. Residents vs Visitors Toggle

Added after the pivot.

  • Positioned on the main page after realizing EMs needed to differentiate populations for more accurate insights.
  • Enhanced outreach strategy decisions based on population type.
Residents vs visitors toggle screenshot

5. Best Practice Overrides Familiarity

The original idea to tie search to each graph (inspired by other platforms) was reconsidered. Page-wide search provided faster, more consistent results.

05 — Impact

What it changed

  • Critical insights are now surfaced upfront, reducing time to interpret data.
  • Navigation and hierarchy improvements allow EMs to focus on decision-making.
  • Page-wide search and residents vs visitors toggle improve accuracy and actionable insight.
  • The platform is scalable for future features without overcomplicating the interface.
Full dashboard screenshot highlighting the redesigned interface
06 — Conclusion

Conclusion

ResiliencePoint illustrates the power of iterative, user-centered design. By observing EM workflows, identifying pain points, and pivoting to a cleaner, more efficient interface, we delivered a platform that balances rich data with clarity. The redesign laid the foundation for EMs to make faster, better-informed decisions, while keeping room for ongoing evolution.
