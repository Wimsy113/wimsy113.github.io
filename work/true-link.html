True Link

Financial tools built to protect the safety and independence of aging and disabled adults without stripping away their autonomy.

Role
UX Designer
Team

My work at True Link spanned both sides of the platform: internal workflow tools that let trust administrators manage disbursements safely and efficiently, and the beneficiary-facing experience that makes that same money understandable to the people it belongs to.
